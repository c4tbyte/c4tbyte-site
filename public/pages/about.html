All About

Hi, I'm Iris — C4TBYTE is my online nickname. It's just me behind all of this.

I am a nerd by profession. I engineer some of the largest AI/HPC infrastructure systems being deployed today — NVIDIA's GB200 and GB300 platforms, thousands of GPUs, the kind of hardware most people will never see in person. When cutting edge technology breaks, there's usually not a manual. Sometimes you get to be the first person who figures it out. It's rewarding and demands a level of curiosity that I've always loved. Being a girl engineer in HPC is also a pretty solid flex.

I love engineering, but somewhere between root cause analysis and optimizing everything in sight, I started missing a different kind of creativity. Making things purely because they look cool. No corporate bureaucracy, no performance metrics, and no need to justify my taste. Just me, my ideas, and working with cool people to turn visions into realities.

That's why I started C4TBYTE. It's a platform for me to explore the intersection of art and technology through web design, development, visual experiments, and whatever else catches my attention. My projects are all over the place, but I spend a lot of my time working with bands, record labels, and music festivals. The hardcore scene has given me a lot over the years, and I have finally found my niche for giving back. I'm also passionate about digital privacy, anti-surveillance, and generally being suspicious of technology that watches us.

TLDR: I like computers. I like making things. I like making things on my computer.
